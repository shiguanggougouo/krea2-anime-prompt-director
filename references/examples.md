# Reference Examples

These are demonstrations of the skill's target quality and workflow, not fixed templates to reuse verbatim. Each example uses one coherent art direction and gives the environment concrete visual structure. Adapt all details to the user's current request.

For action-led images and multi-character exchanges, also use the staging principles in `action-direction.md`. The examples below illustrate one frozen story beat with clear body mechanics, environmental cause and effect, or reciprocal character reactions.

## Example 1 — Canon character in a recognizable world

**User idea:** 旗木卡卡西

**POSITIVE:**
A cinematic 2D shonen-anime key illustration of Hatake Kakashi from Naruto, identifiable by his unruly silver hair swept into sharp spikes, a Leaf forehead protector slanted over his left eye, one relaxed visible eye, the black mask covering his nose and mouth, and the high collar and segmented pockets of his green flak jacket over a dark undersuit. He sits sideways on the curved ridge of a tiled rooftop in the Hidden Leaf Village, casually reading a small orange-covered paperback, his shoulders loose but his visible eye still observant. Keep the forehead protector, eye, and mask unobstructed. One gloved hand steadies the book against the breeze while his other rests near his raised knee; the loose edge of his vest and a few silver hair tips lift in the same wind. Soft leaves frame the near corner of the image, slightly out of focus. Below him, timber balconies, narrow shopfronts, hanging signs, ceramic roof tiles, and a few warm paper lanterns form layered streets that recede toward the Hokage Monument carved into the distant mountain. A painted awning and rain-dark wooden stairway lead the eye down toward the village street, while the rooftops leave a clear gap around the monument. Recent rain darkens the tiles and leaves tiny amber reflections along their edges; late-afternoon sunlight slips through broken clouds and catches the tips of his hair, while cool green-blue shadows hold the village together. Let the focus fall gently from Kakashi's sharply drawn eye and gloved hand to the receding street, keeping the distant monument readable through the roofline gap. Fine ink contours define the hair spikes, mask folds, vest pockets, and book edge; softer painted gradients reserve the atmosphere for the sky and mountain. Clean, confident cel-painted forms, carefully controlled linework, and a restrained green, slate, and warm-amber palette create a quiet moment of watchfulness rather than a posed character sheet.

**中文对照：**
一幅具有电影感的二维少年热血动漫关键插画，描绘《火影忍者》中的旗木卡卡西。他蓬松的银发向上分成清晰尖束，木叶护额斜压在左眼上方，一只放松却警觉的眼睛露在外面，黑色面罩遮住鼻子和嘴；深色紧身内搭外是带高领与分区口袋的绿色上忍马甲。他侧坐在木叶村一处弧形瓦顶的屋脊上，随意地读着一本橙色封面的平装小书。护额、露出的眼睛和面罩都清晰可见，不被遮挡。一只戴手套的手按住被风掀动的书页，另一只手搭在屈起的膝头；马甲下摆与几缕银发朝同一方向轻扬。虚化的近景树叶从画面一角伸入。屋顶下方，木质阳台、狭窄店面、悬挂招牌、陶制瓦片和几盏温暖的纸灯笼层层排列，街道逐渐延伸至远方山体上雕刻的火影岩。彩绘布篷和被雨淋深色的木楼梯将视线引向村道，层叠屋顶之间则为远处雕像留出清晰空隙。雨水打湿瓦片，在边缘留下细小琥珀色反光；午后阳光穿过云隙，照亮他的发梢，冷绿与蓝灰色阴影则统一了整座村庄。焦点从卡卡西清晰的眼睛与戴手套的手逐渐过渡到向远处收束的街巷，同时让火影岩仍能透过屋顶间隙辨认出来。细密墨线刻画发束、面罩褶皱、马甲口袋和书本边缘，柔和绘画渐变则留给天空与山体，强化近实远虚的层次。清晰沉稳的赛璐珞色块、克制准确的线条，以及绿色、石板灰与暖琥珀色的协调配色，营造出安静警觉的瞬间，而非单纯的人物设定图。

## Example 2 — Shoujo heroine and a classic moonlit scene

**User idea:** 美少女战士，经典月夜场景

**POSITIVE:**
A luminous 2D shoujo-anime key visual of Sailor Moon standing on the edge of a Tokyo rooftop beneath an immense full moon. Her unmistakable blonde hair is gathered into two round odango buns with long twin tails streaming behind her; her blue eyes and youthful round face remain visible as she looks toward the horizon. The white sailor bodice with its blue collar, large red chest bow, blue pleated skirt, long white gloves, red knee-high boots, and gold crescent tiara form a clean, readable silhouette. One gloved hand is raised near her chest bow while the other balances at her side, giving her a poised, ready stance; moonlight picks out the glove seams and the folds of her skirt. In the foreground, a few rooftop railings and windblown cherry-blossom petals create delicate framing without covering her face or tiara. Beyond her, layered Tokyo buildings descend toward a distant river; apartment windows glow in small grids of honey-yellow light, while rooftop antennas and water tanks break the skyline into fine shapes. A rooftop access door and low utility housings sit in shadow near the frame edge, grounding the moonlit scene in a real city roof. Wisps of high cloud cross the moon, making a pale halo that outlines her twin tails, and restrained pink-white starlight glimmers around her boots. A ribbon of cloud leaves the moon's brightest disc unobstructed, while faint reflections from the city add a thin rose tint beneath the rooftop edge. Keep the magical accents sparse and connected to her presence, so the night sky remains open and the costume silhouette stays crisp against it. Use delicate contour lines, elegant pose design, soft luminous gradients, and a carefully limited palette of midnight blue, pearl white, rose pink, and warm window gold. The mood is romantic and resolute, with generous sky space and a clear separation between the heroine and the city.

**中文对照：**
一幅明亮通透的二维少女动漫关键视觉图：美少女战士伫立在东京一座屋顶的边缘，身后是一轮巨大满月。她金色长发分成两枚圆润团子髻与向后飘扬的双马尾；她望向远方，蓝色眼睛与圆润年轻的脸庞清晰可见。白色水手服上衣带蓝色水手领，胸前系着醒目的红色大蝴蝶结，下身是蓝色百褶裙，搭配长款白手套、红色及膝靴和金色新月额饰，整体轮廓一眼可辨。一只戴手套的手抬至胸前蝴蝶结附近，另一只手自然侧展，形成优雅而随时准备行动的姿态；月光勾出手套缝线与裙褶。前景中，少量屋顶栏杆与被风吹起的樱花花瓣构成轻盈的画框，不遮住她的脸和额饰。她身后是层层下降的东京建筑群，远处河流隐约可见；公寓窗户点亮成细小的蜂蜜金色光格，天线与屋顶水箱切分出细致的天际线。画面边缘的屋顶出入口和低矮设备箱藏在阴影中，让月夜场景仍带有真实城市屋顶的空间感。高空薄云掠过月面，形成淡淡光晕，勾勒她的双马尾；靴边有克制的粉白色星光。一缕云恰好绕开满月最明亮的圆面，城市反光则在屋顶边缘下方留下一线微弱玫瑰色。魔法光点要稀疏并与她的存在相关，让夜空保持开阔，也让服装轮廓清晰映衬在月光之中。使用纤细轮廓线、优雅姿态、柔和明亮的渐变，以及午夜蓝、珍珠白、玫瑰粉与窗灯金组成的有限配色。整体浪漫而坚定，天空留白充足，人物与城市层次分明。

## Example 3 — A signature battle technique with readable mechanics

**User idea:** 七龙珠孙悟空，龟派气功

**POSITIVE:**
A dynamic 2D battle-shonen key visual of Son Goku in his ordinary Earth tournament appearance: sharply upswept black hair, strong brows, focused dark eyes, an orange gi with a small turtle-school emblem, deep-blue undershirt, blue wristbands, and blue sash. He plants his compact, athletic frame in a low, twisted stance on the pale stone floor of the World Martial Arts Tournament's raised square fighting ring, gathering a Kamehameha. A red boundary line runs around the ring beneath his feet; his knees brace against cracked tiles as his torso turns sideways and his cupped hands draw together beside his waist around a dense blue-white energy sphere. The sphere is the brightest point in the image; narrow rays leak between his fingers and cast cool highlights across his wrists, cheek, and the folds of his gi. Blue-white ki streams back from his hands, pushing dust and loose grit toward the ring's edge. Beyond the ring, crowded stepped stands curve around the arena; red-painted posts, tournament banners, and a tiled-roof entrance pavilion identify the venue, while distant spectators dissolve into the wind-driven cloud light. Use a classic 1990s battle-shonen cel-animation look, bold contour lines, hard-edged shadow blocks, and a disciplined orange, cobalt, and electric-blue palette. Keep his face, black-haired silhouette, emblem, hands, and energy sphere readable so both the character and technique register instantly.
The low camera angle places the square ring across the lower half of the frame, with the audience rising behind him in layered tiers rather than blending into a generic stadium wall. Stone steps at one corner reveal the platform's height; a small judges' table and a fluttering event pennant sit beyond the red boundary, scaled modestly so they do not compete with Goku. His planted foot, bent knees, twisted waist, and locked hands form a clear line of force toward the unseen opponent. Let the charge lift the edge of his gi and send grit skimming across the tiles, while the crowd leans forward as a soft, lively band of faces and color. Maintain crisp separation between character, ring, and stands, with the energy sphere as the sole brightest focal point.

**中文对照：**
一幅充满动势的二维热血战斗动漫关键视觉图：这是地球武道会场景中的普通形态孙悟空，以向上炸开的黑色尖发、浓眉与专注的深色眼睛为特征，身穿印有龟仙流小徽记的橙色武道服，内搭深蓝上衣，腕缠蓝色护腕，腰系蓝色布带。他紧凑结实的身形压低重心，站在天下第一武道会高起的方形浅色石质擂台上凝聚龟派气功；擂台边缘画着醒目的红色界线，脚下石砖已被震裂。他扭转躯干，双手在腰侧紧紧合拢，掌间包裹着一颗密度极高的蓝白色能量球。能量球是画面最明亮的焦点；细窄光束从指缝间穿出，在手腕、脸颊和武道服褶皱上投下冷色高光。蓝白色气流从双手向后翻涌，把尘土和碎石推向擂台边缘。擂台外侧，层层观众席沿着场地环绕；红色立柱、比赛旗帜和带瓦顶的入口楼阁标明这是正式赛事场馆，远处观众融入被风吹动的云层光线中。低机位让方形擂台占据画面下半部，观众席在悟空身后逐层升高，清晰呈现不同空间层次，而不是一整面模糊的体育馆墙。擂台一角的石阶显示平台高度，红色界线外的小型裁判席与飘动的赛事旗只作尺度参照，不争夺焦点。他踩稳地面、屈膝、扭腰、合掌，身体形成一条指向画外对手的蓄力线；气功掀起衣摆，碎屑沿石砖滑向边缘，观众则化为前倾注视的鲜活色带。采用经典九十年代热血少年动画的赛璐珞风格、鲜明有力的轮廓线、硬边阴影色块，以及克制统一的橙色、钴蓝与电光蓝配色。保持人物、擂台、观众席之间的清晰分层，并让能量球成为唯一最明亮的焦点；他的面部、黑发轮廓、徽记、双手与招式都必须一眼可辨。

## Example 4 — Empty classroom background with a specific anime aesthetic

**User idea:** 京都动画风格，雨后的日本高中教室，不要人物

**POSITIVE:**
Anime background painting of an empty Japanese high-school classroom just after a spring shower, in a Kyoto Animation aesthetic. The camera looks diagonally from the back corner toward tall windows on the left, where rain beads cling to the glass and a pale curtain lifts slightly in the breeze. In the foreground, the scratched corner of a wooden desk, a half-open notebook, and a blue mechanical pencil catch small highlights. Across the middle ground, orderly desks and chairs form clean receding rows; a chalkboard holds faint erased equations, and a small eraser tray leaves a dusting of white chalk beneath it. Beyond the windows, wet cherry branches, the school athletic field, and the blurred outline of a neighboring building layer into a cool, rain-washed distance. Soft late-afternoon sunlight breaks through the clouds and lays a warm diagonal across the polished floor, contrasting gently with blue-gray window shadows and tiny reflections in shallow puddles by the sill. Use precise delicate linework, natural object-level detail, calm cel-painted colors, and a bright but slightly melancholic slice-of-life mood. No characters, no people, no figures.
Add a wall clock above the chalkboard, a neatly pinned class timetable, and a few colored magnets on the window frame to make the room feel specific without suggesting anyone is present. The nearest desk is larger and sharper than the rows behind it; chair legs repeat as measured verticals toward the far wall. Tiny droplets trace the window frame and gather at the sill, where a folded cleaning cloth rests beside a watering can. Keep the warm sun stripe narrow and believable, with reflected light softly catching desk edges while the corners remain cool. Preserve generous open floor and quiet air, letting the rain-washed view and the geometry of the room carry the mood.

**中文对照：**
一幅描绘春日阵雨刚停时日本高中教室的动漫背景绘画，采用京都动画式美术气质。镜头从教室后方的角落斜向左侧高窗望去，玻璃上还挂着细小雨珠，浅色窗帘被微风轻轻掀起。前景中，带有刮痕的木桌一角、半开的笔记本和一支蓝色自动铅笔捕捉到细碎的高光。中景里，课桌与椅子整齐排列成向远处收束的行列；黑板上残留着刚被擦淡的公式，粉笔槽下方落着一层白色粉笔屑。黑板上方挂着圆形挂钟，墙面钉着整齐的课程表，窗框上几枚彩色磁贴让空间更有生活感，却不暗示有人入镜。最近的一张课桌清晰而突出，后方桌椅逐渐缩小，椅腿形成规律的竖向节奏。窗外，湿润的樱花枝条、学校操场以及邻近建筑的模糊轮廓，在被雨水洗净的冷色远景中层层展开。细小水珠沿窗框滑落，在窗台边聚成一线，旁边放着折叠的抹布和浇花壶。傍晚前的柔和阳光穿过云层，只在光洁地板上铺出一条克制的暖色斜光，桌沿泛起微亮，墙角仍留在蓝灰阴影里。画面采用精细纤柔的线条、落实到具体物件的生活化细节、平静的赛璐珞色彩，营造明亮却略带怅惘的日常系氛围。地面留有充足空处，雨后窗景与教室几何结构共同形成安静的视觉节奏。画面中没有角色、没有人、没有人物形体。

## Example 5 — A hand-painted rural setting

**User idea:** 吉卜力风格的日本乡间小路，纯场景

**POSITIVE:**
Anime background painting of a narrow rural lane winding between old Japanese homes and deep green hills, in a Studio Ghibli aesthetic. The scene is set in the gentle light of a late-summer morning after a brief shower. In the foreground, moss softens the edges of uneven stone paving, tiny weeds emerge between the cracks, and a shallow roadside ditch carries clear water beneath a small wooden footbridge. The middle ground holds weathered timber walls, faded ceramic roof tiles, a hand-painted shop sign, potted hydrangeas, and a bicycle resting against a low plaster wall. Beyond the houses, layered forested hills recede into soft blue-green haze, while a thin ribbon of cloud catches the sun above the ridge. Light is warm but restrained, filtering through leaves and leaving broken patches across the path; wet surfaces shine gently without looking glossy. Use hand-painted watercolor texture, gentle linework, a natural muted palette, and small imperfections that make the place feel old, quiet, and cared for. No characters, no people, no figures.
Let the lane enter from the lower-left corner and bend behind the nearest house, so the viewer can follow it into the valley. Water in the ditch catches fragments of sky and carries a few fallen leaves beneath the bridge; dark damp grain remains visible in the timber gate, and chipped plaster exposes rough stone beneath. A simple roadside mirror and a weathered utility pole mark the curve without overwhelming the rural calm. Separate the nearest roof, the middle-distance homes, and the far hills with progressively softer edges and cooler color. Sunlight should touch only selected roof tiles and flower heads, leaving most of the greenery in soft shade.

**中文对照：**
一幅描绘日本乡间小路的动漫背景绘画，采用吉卜力式美术气质：狭窄的乡间道路蜿蜒穿过古老民居，通向层叠的深绿色山丘。时间设定在夏末一场短暂阵雨后的清晨，光线柔和安静。前景中，青苔包裹着不规则石板路的边缘，细小杂草从砖石裂缝里探出，路边浅沟里的清水从一座小木桥下流过。小路从画面左下方进入，在最近一幢房屋后方转弯，视线沿着它延伸进山谷；沟水映出断续天空色彩，几片落叶从桥下漂过。中景由风化的木质墙面、褪色的陶瓦、手绘店铺招牌、盆栽绣球花，以及靠在低矮灰泥墙旁的自行车组成。木门上仍能看见被雨水浸深的木纹，剥落的墙皮露出粗糙石基层；弯道旁的道路反光镜和旧电线杆提示着道路走向，却不破坏乡村的宁静。民居之后，层层森林覆盖的山丘逐渐退入柔和的蓝绿色雾气，一缕薄云在山脊上方接住阳光。近处屋顶、中景房舍和远山的边缘逐级变软、颜色逐渐转冷。光线温暖却克制，只照亮几片瓦与花瓣，穿过树叶后在小路上留下斑驳光点；湿润表面呈现自然微光，而非塑料般反射。使用手绘水彩质感、温和线条、自然低饱和配色，以及古老、宁静且有人细心照料的细微磨损。画面中没有角色、没有人、没有人物形体。

## Example 6 — Shinkai-inspired urban twilight background

**User idea:** 新海诚风格，黄昏的日本电车站，纯场景

**POSITIVE:**
Anime background painting of a small Japanese railway platform at blue hour, in a Makoto Shinkai / CoMix Wave Films aesthetic. The sun has just dropped below the horizon, leaving a hyper-saturated band of orange and magenta beneath deep cobalt clouds. In the foreground, rainwater gathered between platform tiles mirrors broken strips of station light, while a yellow tactile paving line leads the eye toward the vanishing point. The middle ground is held by an old timetable case, a vending machine glowing cool white, a modest station sign, and a row of metal benches with tiny droplets along their edges. Beyond the tracks, layered utility poles and fine telephone wires cut across the luminous sky; low residential rooftops fade into violet haze, and a distant train signal burns a single red point near the horizon. The sky is the emotional center of the frame, reflected in wet steel and puddles rather than buried behind decoration. Use crisp hand-painted cloud edges, vivid atmospheric gradients, precise urban silhouettes, and controlled light bloom. No characters, no people, no figures.
Frame the platform from just above bench height, with the tactile paving and rail lines converging toward a small shelter at the far end. A platform clock, route map behind fogged glass, and worn bench slats provide quiet human traces without placing anyone in view. Wet steel catches thin orange reflections on one side and cold blue station light on the other; small puddles break the reflections into uneven bands. The signal's red point should remain distinct from the larger sunset colors, while a faint overhead lamp adds a modest pool of light near the timetable case. Keep the skyline low enough to preserve the broad sky as the emotional center, and let the telephone wires guide the eye across it.

**中文对照：**
一幅描绘蓝调时刻日本小型电车站的动漫背景绘画，采用新海诚／CoMix Wave Films 式美术气质。太阳刚刚沉到地平线下，深钴蓝云层下方仍残留一条高饱和的橙红与洋红色天空。镜头略低于长椅高度，黄色盲道与铁轨线条共同收束到月台尽头的小候车棚。前景中，月台砖缝里的雨水映出断续的站灯光带，一条黄色盲道将视线引向消失点。中景由老式时刻表玻璃框、发出冷白光的自动贩卖机、简朴的车站站牌，以及边缘挂着细小水珠的金属长椅构成；站台时钟、雾气朦胧的路线图和磨损的椅板留下安静的人类活动痕迹，但画面里仍没有人。湿润钢轨一侧映出细窄橙光，另一侧接住冷蓝站灯，浅水洼把倒影切成不规则光带。远处信号灯的红点与大面积夕色清楚区分，时刻表旁一盏顶灯在地面投下克制的小片暖光。铁轨另一侧，层层电线杆和纤细电话线横切明亮天空；低矮住宅屋顶逐渐隐入紫罗兰色薄雾。天空保持宽阔，是画面情绪中心，电线引导视线横穿天际。采用边缘清晰的手绘云层、鲜明大气渐变、精确城市轮廓和克制光晕。画面中没有角色、没有人、没有人物形体。
## Variant selection mini-examples — same character, different story moment

These short anchors demonstrate how the requested moment changes the character design. Keep the chosen version internally consistent throughout the prompt.

### Son Goku — Namek transformation vs. ordinary Earth scene

**Namek / Super Saiyan:**

**POSITIVE:**
On Namek, Son Goku has transformed into a Super Saiyan: his black hair has become sharply upright golden hair, and his turquoise eyes and intense expression contrast with the familiar orange gi, blue undershirt, wristbands, and sash. Show the golden aura separating him from Namek's alien sky; keep this transformed hair and eye color consistent throughout the scene. Frame him against the planet's green sky, distant rocky ridges, and still water so the new form reads clearly without changing his familiar clothing.
